**Terminals on the maps**: see where the faction terminals are and which USB device hacks each one.

**MAPS** (app and web)
- The terminals you can hack are on the maps (silver, with a screen): 2 IGC on Delta, 2 UICS on Quarry, 3 UICS on
  Outskirts, 2 VLF in the Bunker. A new *Terminals* filter shows or hides them.
- Hover (or tap) a terminal: its faction and the **USB hacking device** that hacks it (the device of the terminal's
  faction: IGC, UICS or VLF), with its icon and its market price.
- The help text of the left panel no longer runs over the last filters on small windows.

If you have v1.5.0 or later, the **UPDATE AVAILABLE** button next to the tabs installs this version in one click.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds, hideout progress and settings are kept. Game data: version 1.4.0.3
(2026-11-12). Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).
